TECHNOVA IT SUPPORT GUIDE

Password Reset

Users who forget their password can reset it through the self-service password reset page.

Account Lockout

An account is locked after five unsuccessful login attempts. Users should wait 15 minutes before trying again or contact the IT support team.

Support Hours

The IT support team is available Monday to Friday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Critical Issues

Security incidents, suspected data breaches, and major service outages should be reported to the IT support team immediately.

Hardware Requests

Employees who need a replacement laptop or other hardware should submit a hardware request to the IT support team.

Response Time

Normal support requests should receive an initial response within 24 hours.

Escalated issues should receive an initial response within 4 hours.
