CUSTOMER SUPPORT FAQ

Billing

Customers who believe they were charged incorrectly should contact the billing support team with their account details and transaction information.

If a customer reports a duplicate charge, the billing team should review the transaction before any refund is approved.

Refund Policy

Refund eligibility depends on the specific transaction and account circumstances.

The support system must not promise a refund unless the knowledge base confirms that the customer is eligible.

If the available information is not sufficient to determine refund eligibility, the case should be escalated to a human support agent.

Technical Support

For login problems, customers should first verify that they are using the correct account credentials.

If the problem continues, the issue should be reviewed by the technical support team.

Critical Issues

Possible data loss, service outages, and security breaches must be escalated to a human support agent.

Customers who have contacted support more than three times within seven days should also be escalated for further review.

Feedback

Customers can submit feedback, suggestions, and recommendations about the service. Feedback should be recorded and forwarded to the appropriate team for review.
